# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Giving and gracefully accepting feedback on code and designs.
- Assuming good intent and asking clarifying questions.
- Focusing on what is best for the users and the project.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, or trolling.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces (GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions,
and commits) and in public spaces when an individual represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer through a private channel: open a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/wllamasr/minecraft-server-mgr/security/advisories/new)
on GitHub and mark it as a conduct report. Reports are reviewed and handled confidentially.
The maintainer may take any action they deem appropriate, from a warning to a permanent ban
from project spaces.
